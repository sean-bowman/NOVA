## f1StaticFiringPlume.jpg

- **Subject:** Rocketdyne F-1 engine (unit 104-4) static firing at Edwards Air Force Base. The exhaust plume shows the dark, fuel-rich, unburnt band adjacent to the nozzle exit before it transitions to the bright orange afterburning plume: the visual signature of the F-1 fuel-film-cooling boundary layer.
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:F-1_Engine_Test_Firing.jpg
- **Author/Credit:** NASA Marshall Space Flight Center
- **License:** Public domain (NASA, US Government work)
- **Downloaded:** 2026-07-20

## merlinNozzleChannels.jpg

- **Subject:** SpaceX Merlin 1D engine manufacturing floor. The copper nozzle in the foreground has the axial regenerative cooling channels machined into it, awaiting the outer metal jacket. Per the source description, the channels run down and back up the nozzle to keep it from melting: a channel-wall alternative to the older brazed-tube construction.
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Making_a_SpaceX_Engine.jpg
- **Author/Credit:** Steve Jurvetson
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 (CC BY 2.0)
- **Downloaded:** 2026-07-20
